SOUND EFFECTS:
==============

effetto_raccolta.mp3 - Player collecting items
> Short, satisfying "pickup" sound like collecting coins or gems
> Duration: 0.5-1 second
> Feel: Positive, rewarding, "ding" or "chime" sound

effetto_gatto_raccolta.mp3 - Cat with collection ability helping
> Similar to regular collection but with a cute cat meow mixed in
> Duration: 0.8-1.5 seconds  
> Feel: Adorable, helpful, cat purr + collection sound

effetto_gatto_attacco.mp3 - Combat cat attacking enemies
> Cat hiss or fierce meow with sword/claw sounds
> Duration: 0.5-1 second
> Feel: Fierce but cute, battle cry mixed with cat sounds

effetto_monete.mp3 - Gaining money/coins
> Classic coin drop/jingle sound, multiple coins falling
> Duration: 1-2 seconds
> Feel: Wealth, abundance, metallic coin sounds

effetto_livello_up.mp3 - Player leveling up
> Triumphant fanfare, magical chime, achievement sound
> Duration: 2-3 seconds
> Feel: Accomplishment, power increase, celestial/magical

effetto_vittoria.mp3 - Winning a battle
> Victory fanfare, heroic theme, celebration
> Duration: 1.5-2.5 seconds
> Feel: Triumph, success, heroic achievement

effetto_sconfitta.mp3 - Losing a battle
> Sad trombone, failure sound, but not too depressing
> Duration: 1-2 seconds
> Feel: Disappointment but encouraging to try again

VOLUME GUIDELINES:
==================
- All effects should be normalized to similar volume levels
- Music files can be slightly quieter (will be controlled by music volume slider)
- Sound effects should be punchy but not jarring
- Consider the game is played on mobile devices with varying speaker quality

STYLE NOTES:
============
- Fantasy/medieval setting with some modern elements
- Cat-themed game, so cat sounds should be cute and endearing
- Incremental/idle game feel - sounds should be satisfying for repetitive actions
- Avoid overly aggressive or scary sounds (family-friendly)
- MP3 format for maximum compatibility across platforms (Windows, iOS, Android)

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:
=======================
- Format: MP3
- Quality: 128-192 kbps (good quality but reasonable file size)
- Length: Keep effects under 3 seconds to avoid gameplay interruption
- No silence at beginning/end of files
- Mono or stereo both acceptable

This game features an emotional bond system with cats, ancient relics, horror elements, 
and incremental progression through 16+ different areas, so the audio should support 
the immersive fantasy adventure experience while remaining pleasant for extended play.
