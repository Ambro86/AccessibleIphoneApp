🎵 AMBIENT SOUND REQUIREMENTS FOR EPIC ADVENTURE GAME 🎵

===============================================================================
GENERAL SPECIFICATIONS:
- Format: MP3
- Duration: 2-4 minutes (for seamless looping)
- Volume: Medium-low intensity (will be played at 30% volume)
- Quality: 44.1kHz, 128-192 kbps
- Mood: Immersive and atmospheric, not distracting
===============================================================================

🏘️ VILLAGE - ambient_villaggio_uccelli.mp3
Description: Peaceful countryside soundscape with cheerful birds chirping. 
Should include: Multiple bird species (sparrows, robins, distant crows), gentle 
wind through trees, occasional distant cow bell or sheep bleating. Very calm 
and welcoming atmosphere.
Mood: Safe, homely, peaceful morning in a medieval village.

🏠 CELLAR - ambient_cantina_gocce.mp3
Description: Underground cellar atmosphere with water dripping echoes.
Should include: Slow, irregular water drops hitting stone, hollow echo 
reverberations, subtle air movement through tunnels, occasional distant 
scurrying sounds (very subtle).
Mood: Damp, mysterious, slightly claustrophobic but not scary.

🚰 SEWERS - ambient_fogne_topi.mp3
Description: Urban underground with rat activity and flowing water.
Should include: Distant rat squeaking and scurrying, water flowing through 
pipes, dripping sounds, occasional pipe creaking, echo of distant footsteps.
Mood: Unpleasant but adventurous, urban decay.

🌀 ANCIENT LABYRINTH - ambient_labirinto_vento.mp3
Description: Mystical ancient ruins with magical wind currents.
Should include: Wind whistling through stone corridors, mysterious magical 
hums, distant stone grinding sounds, ethereal whispers (very faint), echo 
of footsteps on ancient stone.
Mood: Ancient, mystical, lost in time.

❄️ SNOWY AREA - ambient_neve_vento.mp3
Description: Arctic wilderness with howling winds and snow.
Should include: Strong wind gusts, snow particles hitting surfaces, distant 
wolf howls (very occasional), creaking of frozen trees, subtle footsteps 
crunching in snow.
Mood: Cold, harsh, survival atmosphere.

🌿 WILD JUNGLE - ambient_giungla_animali.mp3
Description: Dense tropical jungle teeming with wildlife.
Should include: Exotic bird calls, monkey chatter, insect buzzing, leaves 
rustling, distant roars, water trickling, occasional branch snapping.
Mood: Wild, dangerous, full of life and mystery.

🌲 DEEP FOREST - ambient_bosco_foglie.mp3
Description: Enchanted old-growth forest with nocturnal sounds.
Should include: Owl hooting, leaves rustling in wind, distant wolf howl, 
branches creaking, small animals scurrying, gentle wind through tall trees.
Mood: Magical, ancient, slightly eerie but beautiful.

⚰️ CEMETERY - ambient_cimitero_spettri.mp3

Should include: Mournful wind, distant chain rattling, gate creaking, 
ethereal whispers, leaves blowing across stone, distant thunder rumble.
Mood: Spooky, supernatural, respectfully eerie.

🏚️ HOUSE OF HORRORS - ambient_orrori_porta.mp3

Should include: Doors creaking and slamming, floorboards groaning, whispers 
in walls, distant screams (very faint), wind through broken windows, 
occasional unexplained knocking.
Mood: Terrifying, unsettling, psychological horror.

🏭 ABANDONED FACTORY - ambient_fabbrica_macchinari.mp3
Description: Industrial decay with malfunctioning machinery.
Should include: Steam hissing, metal clanking, broken machinery stuttering,

Mood: Post-apocalyptic, industrial decay, mechanical chaos.

⛏️ DEEP MINE - ambient_miniera_picconate.mp3
Description: Underground mining operation with distant work sounds.
Should include: Distant pickaxe strikes on stone, cart wheels on tracks, 
water dripping in caverns, cave wind, occasional rock fall, miner voices 
echoing distantly.
Mood: Claustrophobic, industrial, underground adventure.

🌙 CURSED CRYPT - ambient_cripta_magia.mp3

stone grinding, magical energy crackling, distant chanting echoes,

Mood: Dark magic, ancient evil, mystical dread.

🌊 SEA - ambient_mare_onde.mp3

Should include: Waves crashing on shore, seagulls crying, wind over water, 
ship bells in distance, water lapping against dock, occasional splash.
Mood: Adventurous, open, freedom of the seas.

🏔️ SACRED MOUNTAIN - ambient_montagna_vento.mp3

🌋 ACTIVE VOLCANO - ambient_vulcano_lava.mp3

👑 FINAL PALACE - ambient_palazzo_eco.mp3
Description: Grand royal palace with majestic acoustics.

Mood: Royal, majestic, final destination grandeur.

🌌 DREAM REALM - ambient_sogni_magia.mp3

🍞 EATING EFFECT - effetto_mangiare.mp3
Description: Satisfying eating sound for when player consumes food.
Should include: Crunching, chewing, swallowing sounds. Pleasant and appetizing.
Not too loud or disgusting, just satisfying "nom nom" sounds.
Mood: Satisfying, nourishing, energy restoration.

🧪 POTION DRINKING EFFECT - effetto_bere_pozione.mp3
Description: Magical liquid consumption with mystical undertones.
Should include: Liquid pouring/gulping, subtle magical sparkle, cork pop,
glass/bottle clink, magical energy absorption hum (subtle).
Mood: Magical, healing, mystical power flowing through body.

💧 WATER DRINKING EFFECT - effetto_bere_acqua.mp3
Description: Refreshing water consumption, natural and clean.
Should include: Clear water pouring, satisfying gulping, subtle water droplets,
refreshing "ahh" feeling. Natural and pure, no magical elements.
Mood: Refreshing, natural, thirst-quenching, revitalizing.

===============================================================================
TECHNICAL NOTES:
- Each file should loop seamlessly (fade in/out at beginning/end)
- Avoid sudden loud noises that could startle players
- Layer multiple sound elements for rich atmosphere
- Balance between recognizable and fantastical elements
- Test for pleasant listening during extended gameplay sessions
- Eating/drinking effects should be short (1-3 seconds) and non-looping
- Effects should be satisfying but not overwhelming
